# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user ("como lo veas más conveniente"). Chosen: static multi-page HTML/CSS/JS, no build step, GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis via CDN; deployable free on Netlify or GitHub Pages.

## Users

Homeowners, small-business owners (salons, shops) and builders in the local area (Calle 70 con Carrera 73C, Colombia) who need doors, windows, mirrors, bathroom glass cabins, handrails and finishes made to measure in aluminum, glass and metal. They browse on a phone, mostly arrive from social media or WhatsApp, compare styles and rough prices, then contact the business to quote.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and catalog site for Metalglass, a local aluminum / metalworking / glass workshop. Success is a visitor who sees what the workshop makes, sees honest starting prices, and taps WhatsApp (or calls) to ask for a quote.

## Positioning

Design, fabrication and installation by one workshop, made to measure, with a transparent price list shown up front. Tagline: "Protegemos tu espacio con estilo". Slogan on the banner: "Ideas en metal, espacios con vida".

## Operating Context

Prices are for the work itself; installation is not charged separately (per the owner, "creo que no se cobra la instalación"; confirm before publishing). Many items depend on measurements and are quoted ("cotizar"). Prices are in COP. Contact is primarily WhatsApp, calls also accepted.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: puertas, ventanas y ventanales, espejos, cabinas de baño (acrílico y vidrio templado), pasamanos (interiores/escaleras y balcones), acabados en casa (cocinas, etc.), muebles/vitrinas.
- Pages: inicio, servicios y precios, galería, contacto.
- Pasamanos have no photos or prices yet: show as "cotizar por WhatsApp" and never invent prices.
- Some provided images are AI-generated examples of what can be made, not finished jobs; the site must label them as "ejemplos / referencias", not "nuestros trabajos".
- Language: Spanish (Colombia).

## Product Principles

1. Prices are shown honestly, with "desde" and "cotizar" where the measure decides the price.
2. WhatsApp is always one tap away.
3. Real work and clearly labeled examples never get mixed up.
4. Mobile first: the audience arrives from a phone.
5. Interactive and tactile (scroll, drag, tilt) in service of seeing the products, not decoration.
